# Suk Ran Flowers — Video Production Script
**Total Duration:** 1:28 | **Clips:** 11 | **Duration per clip:** ~8 seconds

---

## Clip 1 (0:00–0:08) — Origins
Black-and-white vintage film grain, 1960s Seoul. Close-up of a craftswoman's hands delicately arranging flowers on a wooden workbench. Soft natural light through an old window, dust particles floating in the air. Slow dolly-in.

## Clip 2 (0:08–0:16) — The Workshop
Same vintage tone. Wide shot of a small flower atelier, shelves lined with vases, scissors and ribbon on the table. A blurred old Seoul street visible through the window. Gentle handheld camera drift.

## Clip 3 (0:16–0:24) — Hotel Shilla Begins
Transition from black-and-white to muted sepia color. Grand hotel lobby with crystal chandeliers. Staff arranging a large floral wedding arch. Warm golden lighting, slow crane shot rising upward.

## Clip 4 (0:24–0:32) — Royal Wedding
Sepia deepening into soft color. Elegant banquet hall, bride and groom silhouetted beneath a floral arch. Time-lapse of an enormous flower installation being completed petal by petal.

## Clip 5 (0:32–0:40) — State Dinner
Formal, dignified atmosphere. Close-up of an exquisite centerpiece on a state dinner table, fine china and candlelight. Blurred silhouettes of dignitaries in the background. Slow, respectful push-in.

## Clip 6 (0:40–0:48) — 1988 Olympics
Full color now. Sweeping wide shot of a stadium opening ceremony, massive floral installations in Olympic ring colors, flags waving, crowd energy. Dynamic aerial camera move.

## Clip 7 (0:48–0:56) — The Award
Close-up of hands receiving a trophy, a quiet proud smile, soft applause blur in background. Cinematic shallow depth of field, warm interior lighting.

## Clip 8 (0:56–1:04) — New Beginning, Hannam-dong
Glass doors opening to reveal a sleek modern flower shop, sunlight flooding in, fresh flowers neatly displayed on minimalist shelving. Smooth steadicam walk-in.

## Clip 9 (1:04–1:12) — Present Day Craft
Vivid, saturated present-day color. Close-up of a florist's hands assembling a delicate wedding bouquet, petals falling in slow motion. Soft focus, shallow depth of field.

## Clip 10 (1:12–1:20) — Everyday Devotion
A delivery van loaded with corporate anniversary wreaths, a celebratory orchid arrangement placed in a bright office lobby. Natural handheld movement, documentary feel.

## Clip 11 (1:20–1:28) — Closing
Slow fade to the brand mark "Suk Ran Flowers" over a soft-focus arrangement of white orchids. Gentle light flare. Tagline fades in beneath: "45 years, devoted to flowers."

---

## Usage Notes
- Paste each clip prompt one by one into Sora 2 (set duration to 8s per clip)
- Assemble completed clips in order using a video editor (CapCut, Premiere, etc.)
- Recommended background music: classical orchestra or piano
